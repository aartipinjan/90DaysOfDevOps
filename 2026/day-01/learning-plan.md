Q 1 . What is your understanding of DevOps and Cloud Engineering?

Answer: DevOps is a way of working where the Development and Operations teams work together. 
The main goal is to deliver software faster, with fewer errors, using automation.
Cloud Engineering is about creating, managing, and maintaining applications and servers
on cloud platforms like Azure, AWS, or Google Cloud. It helps companies build secure, scalable, and cost-effective systems.

Q 2. Why are you learning DevOps and Cloud?

Answer: I have more than 9 years of experience as a Linux System Administrator.
I have worked on Linux servers, troubleshooting, patching, monitoring, and production support.
I want to grow my career because most companies now use cloud and DevOps technologies. 
My Linux knowledge gives me a strong foundation, and by learning DevOps and Cloud,
I can work on automation, CI/CD, containers, Kubernetes, and cloud platforms.
